Privacy Policy

This explains what Avika collects, why, and how it's protected — written for the parents and family members who use the app, in plain language.

On this page

1 Who Avika is for

Avika is a directory and support app for families of children with disabilities in Bengaluru — built by and for parents, and used by adults on behalf of their children. Accounts are created by a parent or guardian, who must be old enough to enter into this agreement under Indian law. Avika is not directed at children, and children do not create accounts or sign in themselves.

2 What we collect

We only collect what the app's features actually need. Browsing the Clinic Directory, Sensory-Friendly Venues, and Schemes & UDID Guide requires no account and no personal information at all.

If you choose to sign in and set up a family account, we collect:

InformationWhy we ask for it
Phone numberTo verify it's you, via a one-time code (Firebase Phone Authentication). We don't see or store the code itself — only that verification succeeded.
Family name & invite codeSo the parents and siblings you invite land in the same shared family group.
Child's name & date of birthTo personalize the app and route you to age-appropriate guidance.
Intake questionnaire answersWhat you tell us about your child's development, in whichever categories you choose to answer — stored so you don't have to re-explain it to every specialist you see.
Basic app usageWhich screens get used, via Firebase Analytics, so we know what to improve. Not linked to advertising of any kind — Avika has no ads.

3 Why we collect it

Every field above exists to make one of the app's features work — signing in, sharing one child's information across a family, or building a picture of your child's needs to make your next specialist visit faster. We don't collect data speculatively for the future, and we don't build advertising profiles.

4 Data about your child

This is the most sensitive information Avika handles, and we treat it that way.

Your child's name, date of birth, and intake questionnaire answers are entered by you, a parent or guardian, and are visible only to the members of your family group — never to other Avika users, and never used for advertising.

The intake questionnaire is deliberately not a diagnostic tool: it doesn't produce a score, a risk level, or a label. It exists to help you describe what you're observing, in your own time, so you have it ready when you see a specialist. Only a qualified professional can diagnose your child.

5 Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. The only third party involved is Google Firebase, which hosts our sign-in and database infrastructure as a data processor — it stores the data on our behalf, under our security rules, and doesn't use it for its own purposes.

We may disclose information if required by Indian law, or to protect the safety of a child where there's a genuine, good-faith concern.

6 Storage & security

Data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure. Access to a family's data — including any child profile and intake answers — is restricted by database security rules to the signed-in members of that specific family group. No other Avika user or account can read it.

7 Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time from the profile screen.
  • You can ask us to delete your family's account and all associated data — see Contact us below. We don't yet have a self-service delete button in the app; until we do, we'll act on email requests promptly.
  • You choose which intake categories to answer, if any — none of it is required to use the Directory, Venues, or Schemes guide.

8 How long we keep it

We keep your family's data for as long as your account is active, so the app stays useful across visits. If you ask us to delete it, we remove it from our active systems; backups are cleared on our normal backup rotation schedule.

9 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how we handle your data, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for material changes, tell you inside the app.
