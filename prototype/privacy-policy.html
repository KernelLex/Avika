Privacy Policy

The short version: Avika does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Here's the full explanation, in plain language.

On this page
1. What Avika is 2. What we collect 3. Links to other apps & websites 4. Children's privacy 5. App permissions 6. If this changes 7. Contact us

1 What Avika is

Avika is a directory and guide for Bengaluru families raising a child with a disability: a Clinic Directory, a Sensory-Friendly Venues list, and a Schemes & UDID Guide. All of this content is bundled with the app itself — there's no account, no sign-in, and no server Avika talks to.

2 What we collect

Nothing. Avika does not ask you to create an account, does not collect your name, phone number, location, or any other personal information, and does not use analytics, ads, or tracking of any kind.

Every screen — the Clinic Directory, Sensory-Friendly Venues, and Schemes & UDID Guide — reads from a list bundled inside the app. Nothing you do in Avika is sent anywhere.

3 Links to other apps & websites

A few buttons in Avika hand off to other apps on your phone:

  • Call opens your phone's own dialer with a clinic's number pre-filled — Avika doesn't place the call or see your call history.
  • Maps opens your phone's default maps app with an address — Avika doesn't know which maps app you use or what happens after it opens.
  • Official portal links on the Schemes & UDID Guide open government websites (UDID, Niramaya, Indian Railways, and similar) in your browser.

Once you leave Avika through one of these, you're subject to that app or website's own privacy policy, not this one.

4 Children's privacy

Avika is intended for use by parents and caregivers browsing information on behalf of a child. The app does not collect any information about you or your child, so there's nothing to disclose here beyond that.

5 App permissions

Avika requests no special Android permissions. It doesn't access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or internet connection directly.

6 If this changes

If a future version of Avika adds features that do collect information — such as accounts or saved profiles — we'll rewrite this policy to describe exactly what's collected and why, update the date at the top of this page, and tell you inside the app before you're asked to provide anything.
