Avika is a free directory and guide for Bengaluru families raising a child with a disability — built to answer three questions parents ask constantly: which clinic, which places are actually sensory-friendly, and which government schemes are we missing out on.

CLINIC DIRECTORY
Browse developmental pediatricians, therapy centers, and specialists across Bengaluru. Each listing includes what they specialize in, contact details, and a one-tap call or map button — no sign-up, no forms.

SENSORY-FRIENDLY VENUES
A curated list of malls, play areas, theatres, and public spaces in Bengaluru that are genuinely welcoming for sensory-sensitive kids, with the details that matter: noise levels, crowding, and quiet spaces.

SCHEMES & UDID GUIDE
Plain-language explanations of the government schemes, benefits, and the UDID (Unique Disability ID) process families in Karnataka and India are entitled to, with links straight to the official portals.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
Avika works entirely offline. There's no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics — every screen reads from a list bundled inside the app, and nothing you do in Avika is ever sent anywhere.

Avika is an independent project, not affiliated with any government body or hospital. Listings are provided as a directory only, not as medical advice or an endorsement.
